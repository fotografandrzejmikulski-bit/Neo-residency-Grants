## Applicant information still required from the applicant

The following must come from the applicant and must not be invented:

- current student status and institution, if applying as a student;
- current city/country and confirmation of North America eligibility where applicable;
- GitHub/portfolio/website links;
- education and technical background;
- previous projects, employment, awards, publications, or other evidence the applicant wants considered;
- any required application-specific demographic/eligibility fields;
- exact answer to any new questions introduced by the next Neo application form.

## Final submission procedure

1. Open the current official Neo Residency portal.
2. Confirm whether the applicable application window is open.
3. Re-check eligibility, geography, dates, funding, and all application questions.
4. Paste the final application narrative from `docs/10-application-draft.md` and adapt to the exact form fields.
5. Use the public repository as the technical evidence link.
6. Do not include claims whose evidence is not present in this repository or a cited primary source.
7. Save a PDF/export/screenshot of the submitted application and record the submission date.

## Direct outreach route

For the currently closed 2026 window, Neo's official portal says pre-seed or seed founders should contact the team and that warm introductions are preferred. Use the application narrative and repository as the technical package for that conversation.
