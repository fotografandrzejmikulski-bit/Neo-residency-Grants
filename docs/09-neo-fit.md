## What the project should emphasize

### Technical depth

Aegis is a systems problem involving durability, concurrency, recovery, and observability. That is materially stronger than presenting another application-layer chatbot.

### High agency

The project has a compact MVP that a technically strong solo builder can implement and benchmark. The narrative should show execution through measurable milestones, not grandiose predictions.

### Community contribution

Aegis should be positioned as infrastructure that other technically strong builders can test, critique, and improve. The project should explicitly invite adversarial testing from peers.

### Career/founder optionality

Neo explicitly says students need not feel pressure to drop out or incorporate. The repository should therefore avoid presenting immediate incorporation as a program requirement. citeturn657288search0turn657288search3

## Claims to remove or qualify from the original research draft

The following should not be presented as established facts without independent evidence:

- a guaranteed or nearly guaranteed investment outcome;
- any claim that the project is "impossible to reject";
- claims about Neo acceptance rates unless sourced;
- claims about specific competitor terms unless verified from current primary sources;
- claims about other companies' valuations, acquisitions, or fundraising that are not necessary to the application;
- claims that Neo's participant recruiting channels produced a particular percentage of hires at OpenAI;
- claims that Aegis automatically reduces HBM/KV-cache usage;
- claims that specific Neo mentors will be assigned to the project;
- claims about exact legal or incorporation timing as a Neo requirement.

## Application principle

The strongest application is not the one with the largest number of market claims. It is the one in which the reviewer can quickly see:

1. a technically serious problem;
2. a precise architecture;
3. a credible solo-builder execution path;
4. a measurable benchmark;
5. evidence of user pain;
6. a clear reason Neo is uniquely useful at this stage.
