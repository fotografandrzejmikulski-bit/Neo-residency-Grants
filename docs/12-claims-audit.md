## Claims removed or reframed

The supplied draft contained several statements too strong to retain as facts without additional primary evidence. They are now reframed as hypotheses, planning assumptions, or explicit items for verification.

### Acceptance-rate claims

Do not state that Neo's acceptance rate is below 1% unless an authoritative source is located and cited.

### Competitor economics

Do not rely on the supplied comparison table for current YC, SPC, a16z Speedrun, HF0, or Techstars economics without current primary-source verification.

### Portfolio outcomes

Do not use speculative or unsupported valuations, acquisition claims, or fundraising outcomes to establish product-market fit for Aegis.

### Technical-performance claims

Do not assert that externalizing agent memory automatically reduces KV-cache or HBM pressure. Treat that as a measurable research hypothesis.

### Neo-specific people

Do not claim that a particular Neo mentor will be assigned, or that named companies' engineers will participate, unless the program explicitly confirms it.

## Editorial standard

Every high-impact external claim in application material should be one of:

1. current primary-source fact;
2. measured project result;
3. clearly labeled hypothesis;
4. clearly labeled planning assumption.

Anything else should be removed.
